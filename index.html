StepRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Overview

StepRush is an arcade mobile game developed by Devora Oyun. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be stored or processed when you use the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as high score, coins, unlocked characters, unlocked backgrounds, selected customization options, and settings. This information is used to provide the game experience and is not used to identify you personally by Devora Oyun.

Advertising

StepRush uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process data such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction data, diagnostics, and ad performance data to provide and measure ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses data here: Google Advertising Policy.

In-App Purchases

StepRush currently uses in-game coins and rewards for customization. If paid purchases are added in the future, transactions will be handled through Apple's App Store systems.

Children's Privacy

StepRush does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising may be served by Google AdMob according to the settings and policies configured for the app.

Permissions

StepRush does not require access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location for gameplay.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes, such as when new features, ad settings, or services are added. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information shown on the App Store listing for StepRush.
